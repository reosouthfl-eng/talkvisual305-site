TalkVisual305

TalkVisual305 is a personal content-automation tool that publishes short-form video content to TikTok on a schedule.

This site documents the application for TikTok's developer review process. TalkVisual305 connects to TikTok using the Login Kit (to verify account ownership) and the Content Posting API (to publish pre-produced video content directly to the connected account).

What it does

The developer produces short-form videos (interviews, community stories, real estate and local-business content from South Florida) and uses this tool to schedule and publish them to TikTok automatically, instead of uploading each one by hand.

Who it's for

This is a single-account personal tool operated by its developer. It does not collect, store, or process data on behalf of other users, and it is not offered as a public multi-user service.
