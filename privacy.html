Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

TalkVisual305 ("we," "us," or "our") operates the TalkVisual305 application and website at talkvisual305.net (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Service, including our integrations with TikTok and other social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we request the following permissions (scopes):

Scope Data Accessed Purpose
user.info.basic Display name, avatar URL, open ID Identify your account and display your profile within the Service
video.publish Permission to publish videos to your TikTok account Post video content on your behalf as part of content distribution
video.upload Permission to upload video files to TikTok Upload video files for publishing to your TikTok account

We access only the data covered by the scopes you explicitly approve during the TikTok authorization process. We do not access your TikTok direct messages, follower lists, or any data beyond the approved scopes.

1.2 Information from Other Platforms

The Service may also integrate with YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. Similar OAuth-based authorization is used for each platform, and we access only the data necessary to distribute content on your behalf within the scopes you approve.

1.3 Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly, such as video content you upload for distribution, captions, titles, and scheduling preferences.

1.4 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL
  • IP address (for security and abuse prevention)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and connect your social media accounts via OAuth
  • Publish and distribute video content to your connected platforms on your behalf
  • Display your basic profile information within the Service
  • Improve and maintain the Service
  • Communicate with you about Service-related matters
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

3.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure, including Supabase (hosted on AWS). OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and are used solely to perform authorized actions on your behalf.

3.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted storage of OAuth tokens and sensitive credentials
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized services only
  • Regular review of security practices

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

3.3 Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Service. Specifically:

  • OAuth tokens: Retained while your account is connected; deleted upon disconnection or revocation.
  • Profile information: Retained while your account is active; deleted upon account removal.
  • Content metadata: Retained for operational purposes (e.g., distribution logs) and deleted upon request.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With connected platforms: When you authorize content publishing, your video content and associated metadata are transmitted to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, or other platforms you connect, in accordance with their respective terms and APIs.
  • Service providers: We use third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting, CDN services) that process data on our behalf under contractual obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your data. We will delete your information within 30 days of a verified request, except where retention is required by law.
  • Revoke Access: You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's settings at tiktok.com/setting under "Manage app permissions." You may also revoke access for other connected platforms through their respective settings.
  • Opt-Out: You may stop using the Service and revoke all platform authorizations at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address listed below.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent the authorization flow from completing.

If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Privacy Policy — TalkVisual305

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

TalkVisual305 ("the application") is operated as a personal automation tool by its developer. This policy describes how data is handled.

What we access

When you connect your TikTok account via Login Kit, the application requests the user.info.basic scope to retrieve your username and profile information, used only to confirm the connection succeeded.

What we do with video content

Videos uploaded through the Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish) are sent directly to TikTok's servers and published to the connected account. The application does not sell, share, or use this content for any purpose other than publishing it to the account that authorized the connection.

Data retention

Authentication tokens are stored only as long as needed to operate the connected account's posting schedule. You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately deauthorizes the application.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties except TikTok's own API, which is required to publish content.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
